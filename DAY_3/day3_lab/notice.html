Department of AI and Data Science - Fee Notice 2026

The following course fees are applicable for the current academic year.

  • CS101 Programming Fundamentals: Rs. 12000
  • AI202 Machine Learning: Rs. 18000
  • DS303 Data Engineering: Rs. 15000

Students with a merit scholarship receive a 10 percent reduction on the total fee. Hostel students pay an additional Rs. 4500 as laboratory charges.
